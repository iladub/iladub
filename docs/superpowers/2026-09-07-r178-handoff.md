## 5. The next concrete action

### 5a. ASSERTED — [[R181]]: measure WHERE the three-line join happens, before designing anything

Mechanical, and that is why it is asserted: the site exists, the graph proves the join happens, and
finding it is instrumentation, not judgement. What is known and what is not:

* `headers.py:458` builds every `HeaderNode` from **one** `cells.SourceCell` and takes that cell's
  four bounds. Measured, and it is the only header-node construction with geometry (R180's row
  records the two-site census).
* `cells._cell_from` (`cells.py:96-103`) computes a bbox as `min/max` over **all** its words — a
  correct union. So the joining code is not this, or the box would already cover three lines.
* Therefore the joined `cellText` and the single-line box **do not come from the same place**, and
  which site performs the join is *unmeasured*. `headers.py:62` and `:80` both `" ".join(texts)`
  per column and are the obvious suspects — **suspects, not the answer**. Naming one here from
  reading is the CLAUDE.md § Plan authoring discipline rule 2 failure exactly, and this loop is
  deliberately not doing it.

The cheap instrument already exists and costs ~20 s: `PYTHONPATH=src python3
scripts/unbooked_ink_fate.py corpus/ag-trade/graincorp-stem-2026-07-31.pdf 0` — the orphan count
must fall **9 → 1** (the print banner) and no other page's count may move.

### 5b. PROPOSED — [[R181]]'s remedy fork, and the prediction that decides it

Two remedies, and they are not exclusive: **union the bounds at the joining site**, or **a
containment shape** (nothing enforces box-covers-text in either direction today —
`tab:WrappedCellShape` is bbox ⇒ non-empty text, and R179's `tab:LabelCellPhysicalShape` requires
only the box's *presence*).

**The prediction, and it is the falsifiable part:** widening a label's box is a **geometry** change
with **no reading consequence** — the corpus verdict diff comes back WHOLE-FILE IDENTICAL while the
orphan count falls 9 → 1. The basis is a grep, stated at that strength:

```
$ grep -rn "hasBBox" src/ vocab/          # readers, not writers
src/iladub/feed.py:481                    _bbox_xy — docstring says "an EntryCell's"
vocab/queries/row-group-key.rq:16         ?cell a tab:EntryCell ; … tab:hasBBox ?bb
```

**No site in `src/` or `vocab/` reads a *LabelCell's* bbox.** **What would sink it:** `_bbox_xy` is
a *generic* function — its scope is its call sites, which this loop did NOT enumerate. Enumerate
them before trusting the prediction (superpowers `enumerating-before-claiming` is the skill for
exactly this). And `region_tiles` runs the physical shapes as a **gate** on what may assert at all
(the R19 lesson, `tiling.py:40-46`), so a widened box that starts overlapping an entry could still
move a verdict even with no Python reader.

**And the trap that makes the disposal narrower than it looks:** the population is **one page of
27**. A corpus diff that comes back identical is therefore consistent with the fix working, the fix
doing nothing, and the fix never running. The orphan count on graincorp-stem p0 is the only signal
with any power here — the corpus diff is the *guard*, not the oracle. This is the [[R171]] /
[[R179]] hazard at a third site.
