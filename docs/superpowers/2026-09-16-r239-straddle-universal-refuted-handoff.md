### 5c. PROPOSED — the universal form is unavailable on ANY wide grid, not just these three pages

**Rests on an argument from three pages, and this corpus cannot falsify it.** The `either`-carrier
count reads 44–46 of 46 on all fourteen bfs boundaries: on a grid wide enough to matter the carrier
set *is* the admitted set, while straddling is by construction a minority event. A universal over
nearly-everything, of a property held by a minority, can only be false.

**Why this may be wrong, stated before anyone builds on it:** the corpus has exactly three decoration
pages, at 15, 16 and 20 columns. **The falsifier is a narrow decoration page** — 2 or 3 columns, where
the carrier set could be a genuine subset of the admitted set — and **no such page exists here**, so
the claim is unfalsifiable on this corpus and must not be promoted to an assertion by repetition. If
a remedy is ever designed around grid width, this is the premise to attack first.
