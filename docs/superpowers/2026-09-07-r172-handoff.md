### 5a. PROPOSED — R176, the denominator that moves when a verdict changes

**The prediction:** the score's token denominator is not stable across a verdict change, and the
instability is large enough to matter — **24 words on apple p0 and 28 on p1 leave
`asserted+escalated` entirely** when a band stops escalating and starts being read. The mechanism
is measured and is in R176's row: an escalated band books *every word of every line it holds*
(`src/iladub/etkl/compile.py:744`), an asserted matrix band books only its *data cells'* words
(`compile.py:923`), so row-stub ink is counted by the first and by neither afterwards. The
prediction is that **making label ink count in the denominator moves every score on the corpus**,
and that apple p0/p1 stop reading `1.0`.

**Why PROPOSED and not asserted.** Three things are unmeasured and any of them can sink it.
(1) Nobody has run the change; the corpus-wide score movement is inferred from two pages.
(2) It is not obvious that *counting* label ink is the right repair rather than *excluding* it
from both sides — a row stub is structure the reading DID recover, so booking it as unread would
be its own kind of wrong, and the fork is a design decision this loop deliberately did not take.
(3) Every pinned score in the suite would move, which is a re-baselining exercise of the shape
[[R174]] priced at one test and this would price at many. **Decide the fork before writing
anything**, and say in the spec which of the two readings of "count it" you are building.
